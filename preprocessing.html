A note on lemmatisation
This tool uses simplified, rule-based lemmatisation to demonstrate how different word forms can be reduced to a common base form. It does not analyse grammatical context and may therefore produce incorrect results, particularly for ambiguous words. Review the suggested lemmas and correct them where necessary.
